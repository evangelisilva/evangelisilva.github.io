About Me

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University at Albany, SUNY, where my research focuses on databases and distributed systems — exploring how we can design scalable infrastructures for managing data at massive scale. My work bridges data management theory and systems engineering, with current interests in graph data models, indexing strategies, and real-time data processing frameworks.

Previously, I was a Senior Software Engineer at Sysco LABS, where I developed and optimized large-scale microservice architectures, data pipelines, and synchronization frameworks for enterprise applications. These experiences shaped my drive to build systems that are not only efficient but also resilient, observable, and impactful in real-world environments.

I’m passionate about data-intensive systems, infrastructure research, and building tools that make data faster, smarter, and more accessible.
