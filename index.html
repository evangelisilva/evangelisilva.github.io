About Me

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University at Albany, SUNY, advised by Prof. Jeong-Hyon Hwang. My research focuses on databases and distributed systems — particularly the design and optimization of on-disk and in-memory key-value stores that enable scalable and high-performance data management. My work bridges data management theory and systems engineering, with ongoing interests in storage architectures, indexing strategies, and real-time data processing frameworks.

Previously, I was a Senior Software Engineer at Sysco LABS, where I developed and optimized large-scale microservice architectures, data pipelines, and synchronization frameworks for enterprise applications. These experiences shaped my drive to build systems that are not only efficient but also resilient, observable, and impactful in real-world environments.

I’m passionate about data-intensive systems, storage research, and building tools that make data faster, smarter, and more accessible.
